## [v0.1.0] - 2026-09-07 ([`a3367d6`](https://github.com/grokify/omniroadmap/commit/a3367d6))

### Highlights

- Dolt-backed canonical store with a provider-agnostic sync engine (Aha!, ProductBoard, JPD), a per-tenant fieldmap for provider-sourced prioritization, and a hand-authored augment overlay that survives re-syncs
- Full COMPASS-RICE prioritization pipeline: two-phase (LLM-judge-proposed, PM-confirmed) profile assignment with a claims-backed confidence integrity check, compile-time gating that excludes anything not yet PM-confirmed rather than falling back to a legacy score, and a PM review gate over compiled rankings
- DashForge-backed analytics layer: a GuardSQL catalog/query surface over items, opportunity assessments, profile assignments, and per-profile raw evidence, plus a curated DashboardIR pack (compass-rice-all, moscow-compass-rice, compass-profile, portfolio-overview) servable or exportable to a standalone dashforge-server
- Cobra CLI (library-first, thin wrappers) covering sync/db/status/augment/item/config/ui, plus the COMPASS-RICE scoring surface: assess (list/show/import/set), profile (list/propose/confirm/reject), moscow (get/set), and analytics (export-dashboards)
- prism-roadmap export and rendering: canonical items convert to prism-roadmap's RoadmapItemSet, and opportunity/portfolio reports (including the COMPASS section) render to Markdown and a Marp presentation projection
